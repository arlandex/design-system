Information architecture

Mineral roles

C2 Product Anatomy V8 is the source of truth for information-architecture layout, hierarchy, typography and interaction rules. The Mineral palette is mapped by function, not just lightness. Structural controls use Body Ink and Mineral Stone to communicate hierarchy. They are not focal accents. No white surface is part of this colourway.

Content workflow, case study

Ship the draft before the plan is finished

What changed when we stopped waiting for perfect briefs

Most teams treat the outline as a gate: nothing moves until it's approved. Loosening that one rule is what actually shortened the cycle. Early drafts gave reviewers something concrete to challenge, so missing assumptions surfaced before they became expensive dependencies.

Drafts started circulating a full day earlier, and the plan caught up around them instead of ahead of them. Decisions became easier to sequence, handovers carried more context, and the team could keep moving without pretending the brief was finished.

Wide canvas

Responsive diagrams

Dense information architecture may preserve its horizontal relationships instead of stacking into a misleading order.

  • Keep the hierarchy at a minimum canvas width of 1280px
  • Allow horizontal scrolling on smaller viewports and show a clear overflow cue
  • Keep page-level controls visible outside the scrolling canvas
