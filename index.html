The Blueprint Diagram design system

This system supports both diagram illustrations and structurally similar, information-architecture-led HTML pages. The warm-neutral colourway is the default across the system. The green colourway is a contextual alternative reserved for specific information-architecture pages, such as the published GPT example; it does not replace the default.

Fonts and headers

Type roles

Ink carries headlines. Body copy uses Body Ink (#48494B), the dedicated reading tone between Fog Stone and Ink, so long paragraphs don't sit at full headline weight while still holding proper reading contrast.

Content workflow, case study

Ship the draft before the plan is finished

What changed when we stopped waiting for perfect briefs

Most teams treat the outline as a gate: nothing moves until it's approved. Loosening that one rule is what actually shortened the cycle — drafts started circulating a full day earlier, and the plan caught up around them instead of ahead of them.
